Animator Controls

How to Use:

  1. Upload a field image/PDF & initial robot sprite.
  2. Optional: Create Sprite Groups to layer sprites together (e.g. robot + object).
  3. Optional: Disable background to record with solid color.
  4. Click on the field to add waypoints (even just 1!).
  5. Drag waypoints to reposition them.
  6. Hold SHIFT while dragging for straight lines!
  7. Select a waypoint to edit heading and actions.
  8. In each action, pick Solo sprite or a Group.
  9. Use "Add Sprite Action" or "Duplicate" for stop-motion.
  10. Adjust rotation for each action to rotate sprites.
  11. Adjust playback speed for faster/slower animations.
  12. Press 'Animate' to preview or 'Record' to save.
  13. While recording: Press SPACE to pause, then drag to pan or scroll to zoom!

Setup

Scenes

Each scene plays for a fixed duration. Add waypoints per-track per-scene — tracks with no waypoints in a scene stay still.

Sprite Tracks

Each track is an independent sprite. All tracks animate simultaneously.

Sprite Groups

Groups let you layer multiple sprites at the same position. Perfect for a robot carrying an object — then switch to solo sprite at the 'launch' waypoint.
